## 3. 💡 GTM Ideas — Path to First 50 Users

### Idea 1 — "Catch the Alpha" Challenge on X/Twitter
**Mechanic:** Post a daily AI-generated trade signal for 2 weeks. Followers who apply the signal and share their P&L screenshot (win or lose) get entered into a draw for 3-month Pro access. Top performers earn "AgentVault Verified Trader" status.

**Why it works:** Crypto Twitter loves public trade calls and P&L flexing. Every screenshot shared is organic UGC that exposes AgentVault to hundreds of new accounts. The product proves itself in real-time — the best possible live demo.

> 💰 Cost: ~$0 &nbsp;|&nbsp; ⏱️ Effort: 1 post/day + community replies

---

### Idea 2 — University Blockchain Club Early Access Partnership
**Mechanic:** DM 10–15 blockchain club leaders on LinkedIn with a partnership offer: AgentVault provides the entire club free beta access + a custom invite link, in exchange for hosting a 30-min "AI Trading 101" session (we provide the deck). Club leaders get a referral incentive for every member who signs up.

**Why it works:** One "yes" from a club leader = 50–100 signups in a single session at zero cost. Students are high-quality early adopters who give honest feedback and share tools within tight peer networks.

> 💰 Cost: $0 &nbsp;|&nbsp; ⏱️ Effort: 10–15 LinkedIn DMs + one reusable deck

---

### Idea 3 — "Roast My Portfolio" on Reddit & Discord
**Mechanic:** Post in r/CryptoCurrency, r/AlgoTrading, and relevant Discord servers offering free AI portfolio analysis. Users share their anonymised portfolio or recent trades; the team responds publicly with an AI-generated breakdown — what signals support or contradict their positions. Participants get early access in exchange for honest product feedback.

**Why it works:** Crypto communities love public analysis threads — massive engagement. Every public reply is a live product demo seen by thousands of lurkers. Builds authority, collects user data, and generates sign-ups simultaneously. The "roast" framing is disarming and fits crypto culture perfectly.

> 💰 Cost: $0 &nbsp;|&nbsp; ⏱️ Effort: 1–2 hrs/day moderating for the first 2 weeks

---

> **Common thread across all 3 ideas:** Show, don't tell — let the product prove itself publicly, in front of real users, at zero cost.
